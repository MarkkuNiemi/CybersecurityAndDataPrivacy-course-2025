# Cookie Policy – Booking System 

This Cookie Policy explains how the Booking System uses cookies in accordance with GDPR and common best practices.

## 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on the user’s device when accessing a website. They help the site function properly and maintain user sessions.

## 2. Cookies Used in This Application
The Booking System uses **only one essential session cookie**. This cookie is required for the application to function correctly and is not used for tracking or analytics.

### **Session Cookie (Strictly Necessary)**
- Maintains the user’s login session  
- Ensures secure navigation between pages  
- Contains no personal data  
- Automatically expires when the browser is closed  

According to industry guidelines and GDPR interpretations, session cookies do **not require user consent**.  
(Reference: https://www.cookieyes.com/blog/session-cookies/)

## 3. No Non-Essential Cookies
The application does **not** use:
- analytics cookies  
- marketing cookies  
- third-party cookies  
- tracking technologies  

Therefore, no cookie banner or consent mechanism is required.

## 4. How to Manage Cookies
Users can manage or delete cookies through their browser settings. Disabling session cookies will prevent the Booking System from functioning properly.
